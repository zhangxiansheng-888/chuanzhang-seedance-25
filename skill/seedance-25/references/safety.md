# Safety, rights, and honest observation

> 船长AI视界整理与维护

## Rights gate

- Use a real person's face or voice only with appropriate consent and authorization.
- Use owned, licensed, public-domain, or authorized images, video, music, voices, logos, characters, and product designs.
- When rights are unclear, transfer broad production traits—camera function, motion rhythm, palette, material, genre, or emotional arc—without copying protected identity, voice, logo, or distinctive character design.
- Do not rewrite wording to evade platform safeguards. Offer a safe production alternative that preserves the benign creative goal.

## Real-person and voice work

Confirm authorization when the request involves impersonation, a public figure, private person, cloned voice, deceptive endorsement, or misleading context. Avoid generating content meant to defraud, harass, sexualize without consent, or misrepresent real events.

## Brand and product work

Preserve exact logos, labels, packaging, and claims only when the user is authorized to use them. Keep legal copy and small on-screen text in post when exact accuracy matters.

## Honest observation

Never say an attachment or generated take was watched, heard, measured, or verified unless the current client made it inspectable. If not inspectable:

1. say so briefly;
2. rely on the user's description;
3. mark state as user-reported;
4. ask for only the missing detail that blocks the next step.

Never invent a final frame for extension work; it will corrupt every later continuity instruction.
